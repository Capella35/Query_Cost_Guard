**22/26.** Three misses and one false alarm, all of kinds named in the set's
header as likely before the run:

- **H-J4** (missed): `JOIN ... USING (user_id)`. The rule reads `ON` and
  `WHERE` equalities only; a `USING` column is not checked.
- **H-F5, H-F8** (missed): the join sits in a CTE or a subquery and the SUM
  outside it. The rule looks for the aggregate and the join in the SAME
  SELECT, so a fan-out produced one level down is not seen.
- **H-C5** (false alarm): cost of goods sold, `SUM(p.cost)` over sold items.
  Summing a product's cost once per item sold is the intent here; the rule
  cannot tell "price × quantity" from a double count. This is the rule's
  real limit: a one-side value summed over many-side rows is sometimes right.

What it means for the verdict: both rules warn, never block, so the false
alarm costs a warning the analyst can dismiss; the misses leave the
verdict where it was without the rules. Fixing USING and the one-level-down
cases would make this a tuning set; a further claim needs another fresh set.
